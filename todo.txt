The Harmony prefix on Map.ConstructComponents: on load, Map.ExposeData constructs components before it reads their saved data. So a component's constructor runs while map.generatorDef is still the placeholder CF_CaveMapGenerator — CF_Biome_Error, no real shape. CaveInfo fixes it at PostLoadInit, which is too late for any constructor. Yours are fine (you don't read the def in a ctor), but a third-party CustomMapComponent on someone's cave shape could read map.generatorDef.pocketMapProperties.biome and get garbage. A prefix on ConstructComponents would repair the def before any ctor runs. Still worth leaving in your pocket rather than building.
